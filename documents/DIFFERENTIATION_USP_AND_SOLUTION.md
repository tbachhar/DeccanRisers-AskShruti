## Part 2: How It Solves The Problem

### The Problem: Healthcare Accessibility Crisis

**Root Cause**:
Digital form barrier prevents 500M+ Indians from accessing telemedicine.

**Why Forms Are a Barrier**:

```
Patient wants healthcare
    ↓ (opens eSanjeevani)
Encounters digital form
    ↓ (complexity + English + typing)
COGNITIVE OVERLOAD
    ↓ (confusion, frustration)
Form abandoned
    ↓ (no consultation)
UNTREATED HEALTH ISSUE
```

**The Form Problem Breakdown**:
1. **Time**: 10 minutes of struggling (discourages patients)
2. **Language**: English only (excludes 65% of rural India)
3. **Literacy**: Complex medical terminology (elderly can't understand)
4. **Motor Skills**: Typing is hard for elderly (arthritis, tremors)
5. **Cognitive Load**: Multiple fields to fill (memory issues for elderly)
6. **Verification**: No confirmation system (errors go unnoticed)

---

### How AskShruti! Solves Each Problem

#### 1. **Time Problem**: 10 minutes → 30 seconds

**Before AskShruti!**:
```
1. Open eSanjeevani (1 min)
2. Click "Create Case" (30 sec)
3. Find form, scroll through sections (2 min)
4. Read field labels, understand what's needed (2 min)
5. Think about symptoms, recall medications (2 min)
6. Type information slowly (2-3 min)
7. Review and correct typos (1-2 min)
TOTAL: 10-12 minutes ❌
```

**With AskShruti!**:
```
1. Click AskShruti icon (10 sec)
2. Say symptoms in Hindi (10 sec)
3. AI transcribes and understands (2 sec)
4. Say medical history (10 sec)
5. AI extracts and populates (2 sec)
6. Say medications (10 sec)
7. AI maps to form fields (2 sec)
8. Say allergies (5 sec)
9. Review form (5 sec)
10. Confirm (2 sec)
TOTAL: 30-40 seconds ✅ (20x faster)
```

**Solution Mechanism**:
- Patient speaks naturally (no cognitive processing of form fields)
- AI understands medical terminology instantly
- Form auto-populates while patient reviews
- Patient confirms what they see (not what they typed)
